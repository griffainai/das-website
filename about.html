Driver Appreciation Solutions

The fleet recognition standard
for North American carriers.

3.5 million commercial drivers keep North American freight moving. We built the infrastructure to recognize them — consistently, at scale, and in a way that changes retention outcomes.

Recognition isn’t a perk. It’s a retention strategy.

Driver Appreciation Solutions builds the recognition programs that keep professional drivers — at fleet scale, on a carrier’s own timeline, under the carrier’s own brand.

Our MissionWhy this company exists

Most carriers already know they need a recognition program. What they lack is a partner who can execute one at fleet scale.

The OTR sector runs at 89% annual turnover among large carriers. ATRI puts the cost of replacing a single driver at $8,000–$15,000 — before you account for lost productivity, training hours, or the cascading effect on team morale. These aren’t abstract statistics. They’re line items on every fleet operations budget.

Driver Appreciation Solutions was founded by transportation veterans who understood one thing clearly: the gap is never the intent. It is the right products, the right cadence, and the credibility to do it without it feeling like a corporate afterthought.

What We Stand ForThree principles behind every product we ship

Built for trucking. Not adapted from gifting.

Every kit originates from the trucking profession — not repurposed from a corporate gifting catalog. The products, language, materials, and packaging reflect the specific culture and standards of commercial drivers. That difference is visible the moment a driver opens the box.

Program architecture. Not just products.

We work with VP Safety and operations teams to structure recognition calendars that align with Driver Appreciation Week, service milestones, onboarding, and seasonal programs. The result is a repeatable system — not a one-time order that gets forgotten until next September.

Fleet-grade quality. No exceptions.

The quality of a recognition kit signals what the carrier thinks of the driver receiving it. We hold every product to a single standard: if we wouldn’t be proud to receive it, it doesn’t ship. Premium materials, premium presentation — on every order, at every tier.

How We OperateFleet-scale execution without the friction

We removed the three barriers that keep recognition programs from getting off the ground: long lead times, complex minimums, and pricing you can’t verify until after you’ve committed.

01Transparent, published pricing

Per-unit pricing is visible before you order — across every tier, every product. No hidden fees, no surprise invoices, no NDAs required to see a number.

0210-unit floor, national-fleet ceiling

Programs start at 10 units per product. Volume pricing unlocks automatically at 100+ drivers — same SKUs, same quality, better unit economics.

03Program-managed fulfillment

Standard orders ship per your program timeline, after artwork approval. Express processing is available for time-sensitive programs including Driver Appreciation Week.

04Custom branding on every order

Fleet logo, driver name, and custom message included at every tier. Recognition that looks like it came from the carrier — because it did.
