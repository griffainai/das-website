## 2 · Product cards and the product page — 4:5 portrait

Every card and gallery frame is **4:5 portrait**. Of 54 products only 6 have a native 4:5
photograph; 33 are currently whole only because the build pads them with their own edge
colour, and 15 are cover-cropped. A reshoot at 4:5 removes the padding entirely and is
the single highest-value photography job outstanding.

- **Size:** 2000 x 2500px or larger (4:5). Larger is better — the product page renders
  ~950px wide and only 1 of 54 photographs can currently serve a 2x retina screen.
- **Framing:** product centred with even margin; the frame is filled edge to edge.
