# Touchdown — portal-polish #4

## Aim
Three reported defects: nav logo shrinks on /shop, /account sidebar leaves white space when scrolling long sections, and Ideas & Guides should surface the blog as a program resource.

## What it was told
Jayden, with a screenshot of /account: logo shrink on shop "fix this fully"; sidebar "getting stopped" with blank white space beside tall content; link the blog with post previews in the account Resources.

## What it did
- 6 pages (shop + 5 SEO pages): removed the inline `style="height:36px"` that was overriding the stylesheet's 62px `.nav-logo-img` — the actual shrink cause; all pages now defer to the same rule as index
- account.html: `body{overflow-x:hidden}` → `overflow-x:clip` — hidden creates a scroll container that BREAKS `position:sticky`, which is why the 100vh sidebar scrolled away and left white space; clip clips without a scroll container (same fix ODNDR shipped)
- account.html sec-ideas: "Program Guides — From the Blog" card grid previewing all 6 real blog posts (real titles from their <title> tags, one-line summaries, Read Guide links), above Saved Products; section subtitle updated
- commit: "portal: sticky sidebar fixed, shop logo unshrunk, blog guides in Ideas & Guides" + deploy `vercel deploy --prod`

## How it went
Grade: 4 — all three were diagnosable from source: the logo bug was a grep (six pages carried an inline override the others didn't), the sidebar bug is the textbook overflow-x-hidden-kills-sticky trap with the fix already proven on the sister site, and the blog block reuses the portal's own prog-card grammar so it looks native.
